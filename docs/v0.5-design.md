# v0.5 "Game": design contract

Implements SPEC.md "v0.5 Game" §1–4 plus the interaction rules §11–16 of research-cute-pets.md. This file fixes
names, numbers and file ownership so several people can work in parallel. Shared catalogs already live in
`src/core/types.ts` (`ACCESSORIES` with 20 ids and `ACCESSORY_SLOT`, `SEASONS`, `DECOR_IDS`) and
`src/core/life.ts` (`MILESTONE_ACCESSORIES`, `SEASONAL_ACCESSORIES`, names, emoji, requirements).

## Ownership
- **Core** (`src/core/**`, `src/backend/demo.ts`): pure game modules, LifeState, brain hooks, copy, tests.
- **Art** (`src/pets/**`, `src/gallery/**`): new accessories, decorations, pet acts and pose fields.
- **UI** (`src/overlay/**`, `src/settings/**`, `src/ui/**`, `src/diary/**`): after core and art land.

## Core

### Seasons (`src/core/seasons.ts`)
- `activeSeason(date: Date): { season: Season; start: Date; end: Date; daysLeft: number } | null`.
- Windows (local dates, inclusive):
  - Tết: 7 days before to 7 days after the lunar new year.
  - Trung Thu: 3 days before to 3 days after the 15th of the 8th lunar month.
  - Halloween: Oct 20–Nov 1.
  - Christmas: Dec 15–Jan 1.
- Lunar table, computed with Hồ Ngọc Đức's Vietnamese calendar algorithm (UTC+7):

  | Year | Tết | Trung Thu |
  |---|---|---|
  | 2026 | 02-17 | 09-25 |
  | 2027 | 02-06 | 09-15 |
  | 2028 | 01-26 | 10-03 |
  | 2029 | 02-13 | 09-22 |
  | 2030 | 02-02 | 09-12 |
  | 2031 | 01-23 | 10-01 |
  | 2032 | 02-11 | 09-19 |

  Outside the table there are no lunar seasons.

### Chests (`src/core/chests.ts`)
- **Earning:** a chest every `CHEST_EVERY_SEC = 1800` s of active work. Active work means work hours, not idle (input
  within 60 s), not on a distraction, and not in a meeting. At most `MAX_CHESTS_PER_DAY = 3` are earned per day and
  `MAX_HELD_CHESTS = 3` held at once.
- **Earned:** the pet says so once per chest, in a small silent reply bubble ("🎁 I found a chest! Finish your next
  task to open it"), never counted against the budget. It waits until you're around and nothing else is showing;
  during focus it joins the end-of-session bubble instead. The board's stats row shows a 🎁 pill with the held count.
- **Opening:** finishing a task opens one held chest. The result is part of that task's celebration bubble. If
  Todoist can't close the task, the chest goes back (its XP, snack or golden treat is taken back; an accessory stays).
- **Odds** (`ODDS`, shown in the wardrobe): common 70%, rare 25%, legendary 5%.
  - **common:** +20 XP, or a 🍪 if the stash isn't full (1 in 4 commons).
  - **rare:** a random regular chest accessory you don't own (not a milestone, not seasonal), else +40 XP.
  - **legendary:** an unowned seasonal accessory of the active season, else a golden treat (+50 XP, `goldens` +1).
- A new accessory is put on if its slot is empty, as `checkUnlocks` does.
- `openChest(s, roll, pick, season)` is pure and returns `{ tier, reward: "xp" | "snack" | "accessory" | "golden", accessory?, xp }`.
- LifeState gets `chests`, `chestsToday`, `chestDay` and `workSec`. Undoing a task does not close the chest again.

### Crafting (`src/core/craft.ts`)
- **Progress:** `craft` goes from 0 to 1. It grows only while you work on the session, as chests count work (input
  within 60 s, not on a distraction; `isKnitting`), by `seconds / (max(sessionMinutes, 25) × 60)`.
  `sessionMinutes` is the running session's own length, not `prefs.focusMinutes`. A whole session of 25 minutes or
  more makes one item; a shorter one ("Try 2 minutes", the demo's 1 minute) knits its share. Breaks don't knit. The
  brain counts real elapsed time between heartbeats (a gap counts as 60 s at most) and the last seconds up to 0:00.
- **Finishing:** an item is finished the moment `craft` reaches 1, mid-session if need be. Its line shows then, or
  joins the end-of-session bubble. There is no top-up: a completed session finishes an item only if the progress
  really reached 1, and stopping early keeps the progress. Within 1e-6 of 1 counts as 1 (float sums). At 1 a random
  unowned `DecorId` is added to `decor`, then `craft` resets to 0. Once every decoration is owned, a finished item
  gives +30 XP instead, at most 90 XP a day (`craftXpToday` on `craftXpDay`); after that it's a keepsake (0 XP).
- **Dropped stitches:** 30 s on a distraction during focus costs 0.10, once per distraction episode, never below 0
  and never from a finished item.
- **Exposure:** BrainState exposes `craft`, and the decoration just finished for a reveal bubble.

### Trips (`src/core/trips.ts`)
- **Catalog:** 16 souvenirs `{ id, emoji, place: {vi,en}, item: {vi,en}, postcard: {vi,en} }`:
  - Vietnam: Hội An lantern 🏮, Đà Lạt strawberries 🍓, Hạ Long shell 🐚, Sa Pa postcard 🏔️, Bến Tre coconut candy 🥥,
    Hà Nội egg coffee ☕, Sài Gòn bánh mì 🥖, Huế sesame candy 🍬, Phú Quốc pearl 🦪.
  - Elsewhere: Kyoto fan 🪭, Seoul hotteok 🥞, Bangkok mango 🥭, Paris croissant 🥐, Bali frangipani 🌺,
    Reykjavík aurora card 🌌, Swiss cheese 🧀.
- **Offer:** when today's plan is complete (completed ≥ target and nothing planned is left), in work hours, ≥ 60 min
  before work ends, with no trip yet today and no **Stay** today. A check-in bubble with no `kind` (merged with v0.4:
  only counted reminders have one), so it is never counted against the budget and closing it is not a refusal;
  answering it starts the dismissal run over, as answering any check-in does: "Back at HH:MM",
  **Go!** / **Stay**. **Stay**, or closing the offer, is saved as `tripDeclined` (a day key), so a restart doesn't
  ask again.
- **Away:** `trip = { start, back: min(now + 2 h, workEnd), place }`. While away, only urgent bubbles show. A loaded
  trip is clamped to `back ≤ start + 2 h`; times outside 2020–2200 are junk; a clock earlier than `start` ends it.
- **Return:** `callBack()` ends the trip early; a souvenir comes only after ≥ 30 min away. On return the pet brings
  an unowned souvenir (else +20 XP), writes a diary "postcard" entry and celebrates. One trip per day, but a return
  under 30 minutes doesn't use it up (`tripDay` is cleared and the offer can come again). For those first 30 minutes
  the sign's tooltip and accessible name say "Call me back early and there's no souvenir yet".

### Weekly chapters (`src/core/chapters.ts`)
- `weekKey(date)` is the Monday's day key. LifeState gets `week: { key, done, stickers, trips }` and `lastWeek`.
- The first greeting of a week adds a fresh-start line with last week's recap.
- `weekChapters(entries)` groups diary entries by week with a one-line recap, for the diary page.

### Prefs and rules
- Prefs: `showHome` (true) and `homeItems: DecorId[]` (max 3).
- Every new counted rule has a `kind`.
  - The trip offer is a check-in: no kind, not counted, and held while the pet is "done for today" (v0.4).
  - The chest, craft and trip-return bubbles are replies (no `why`).

## Art (`src/pets`)
- **Accessories:** draw the 14 new ones in the house style at the existing anchors (head, bow, neck, face) for all
  24 species; extend `accessoryTop`.
  - beanie, flowercrown, partyhat, nón lá (`nonla`), bandana, heart, round and star glasses;
  - maicrown (yellow apricot blossoms), tetscarf (red with gold), bunnyband (moon-rabbit ears), witchhat,
    santahat, bellcollar.
- **Decorations:** `renderDecor(id: DecorId, opts?: { size?: number; idPrefix?: string }): string`, a 64×64 viewBox
  each, in the pets' style. One tiny cute detail each, e.g. a fish in the fishbowl or a heart on the mug.
- **New optional `PetPose` fields:**
  - `act?: { kind: PetActKind; t: number }` (seconds since the act began);
  - `craft?: number | null` (knitting: a yarn ball and needles beside the pet plus the growing item);
  - `petting?: number` (seconds of petting: happy squint, lean toward the cursor, faster tail, a slow cat blink after 2 s);
  - `drowsy?: number` (0–1: slower and longer blinks, head dips).
- **Acts:** `PetActKind = "wave" | "hop" | "giggle" | "dizzy" | "pout" | "shake" | "lookaround" | "sniff" | "scratch" | "stretch" | "yawn" | "sneeze" | "trip" | "ponder"`,
  with `ACT_DURATION` in seconds exported for the overlay. Rendering stays pure and fast (< 0.5 ms per frame).
- **Gallery:** `?matrix=acts`, wear rows for the new accessories, and a decorations strip.

## UI
- **Overlay interactions** (research-cute-pets.md §11–16): petting detection, click combos (giggle, then dizzy and
  pout), pick-up stretch and put-down shake, a return greeting scaled by time away, an idle fidget scheduler,
  drowsiness and a yawn, rare sneezes and trips, and `headTilt` eased in when the cursor rests nearby or before
  celebrations.
- **Clicks:** the first click on the pet toggles the board at once; clicks within 0.5 s of the last one are part of
  a burst and never toggle it (a double click opens it once). 3 quick clicks giggle, 6 get dizzy, as before.
- **Ponder:** the `ponder` act lasts 0.4 s (`ACT_DURATION.ponder` = `INTERACT.ponder`), so it eases out instead of
  being cut. The confetti and the chest pop wait for it only when it actually plays.
- **Other overlay pieces:** the home corner (up to 3 decorations beside the taskbar strip; a pet dropped onto it
  lands beside it, with a little hop if it was let go on the ground), the trip sign (clickable, calls the pet back),
  and the chest reveal.
- **Board:**
  - a Home sheet to pick decorations;
  - a souvenir shelf;
  - a 🎁 pill with the chests held in the stats row (opens the wardrobe);
  - chest count, odds and active season (days left) in the wardrobe; each accessory tile shows the current pet
    wearing it (`renderPet`, cached), locked ones softened but still in color;
  - craft progress in the focus bar.
- **Diary:** weekly chapter headers. **Settings:** `showHome`.
